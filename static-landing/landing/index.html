Roadmap

What's shipped and what's coming next for Wenlo.

V1 Shipped

Core Foundation

Auth, workspaces, tasks, and focus essentials

Workspaces & Roles

  • Workspaces: Name, identifier, email invites, and a peps directory
  • Auth: OTP, password, and Google login
  • Role System: permissions

Projects & Tasks

  • Projects: Public workspace and private member access
  • Task Engine: Priorities (P1–P4), due dates, and assignees
  • Subtasks & Statuses: Nested subtasks, To Do, In Progress, Review

Views & Search

  • List & Kanban: Active/done split and drag-and-drop board
  • Smart Lists: Today, Upcoming, This Week, and Overdue
  • Instant Search: Fuzzy search by title and assignee, plus filter bar

Focus & Polish

  • Pomodoro: 25/5/15 timer, stopwatch, and session log
  • Deadlines & Alarms: Request approvals, overdue flags, and app-open alarms
  • Check-in: Daily check-in
V2 IN PROGRESS

Team Collaboration

Collaboration

  • Sync: weekly/daily sync

Power Workflows

  • Command Palette: Lightning actions via Cmd+K / Ctrl+K
  • Eisenhower Matrix: Urgent vs. important quadrant sorting
  • Recurring Tasks: Completion-driven RRULE schedules
V3 PLANNED

Analytics & Automation

Insights, timelines, and power tools

Analytics & Tracking

  • Gamify: Points, tier levels, and habit tracking
  • Time Tracking: Billable hours, team logs, and payroll export
  • Dashboards: Shareable workspace dashboards and scheduled reports

Views & Automation

  • Advanced Views: Gantt, Timeline, and Calendar
  • Power Search: FQL queries and smart input parsing
